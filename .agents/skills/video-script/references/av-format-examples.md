# AV formatting examples for other briefs

These retained examples illustrate AV rows, synchronized visuals, and explicit audio states. They are fictional formatting examples for other creators or explicitly different briefs, not Lazar's current voice or default opening format. Their historical timing, product claims, and numerical results are not verified production facts. Do not reuse them as evidence. For Lazar, apply the main skill's 20–30-second hook → existing 2–3-second MOGRT bumper → body sequence.

## Example 2: Short-Form Instagram Reel — "One Productivity Tip in 30 Seconds"

```
## Script Brief

**Topic:** The 2-minute rule for beating procrastination
**Format:** Instagram Reel / Short-form (30 seconds)
**Key points:**
1. If a task takes under 2 minutes, do it immediately
2. It eliminates the mental pile-up of tiny tasks
**CTA:** Follow for more productivity tips
**Tone:** High energy, punchy, direct
```

```
## HOOK (0:00–0:03)

| VIDEO | AUDIO |
|---|---|
| Tight shot. Speaker snaps fingers on the first beat. Text on screen: *"THE 2-MINUTE RULE"* Burn-in caption: *"THE 2-MINUTE RULE"* | **SFX:** finger snap. **NARRATOR:** "You're procrastinating wrong." |

---

## POINT (0:03–0:22)

| VIDEO | AUDIO |
|---|---|
| Cut to medium shot. Speaker talking fast and direct. As the narrator says "do it NOW," text on screen slams in: *"< 2 minutes? Do it NOW."* Burn-in caption: *"< 2 minutes? Do it NOW."* | **NARRATOR:** "Here's the rule: if something takes less than two minutes — replying to that email, putting away the dishes, sending that invoice — do it NOW." |
| Same shot. | **NARRATOR:** "Not later. Not after lunch. Right now." |
| Cut to B-roll — rapid montage: typing an email, filing a paper into a folder, clicking "send" on an invoice. Three quick cuts, ~1 second each. | *No narration during the montage, ~3 seconds — let the visuals carry the beat.* |
| Cut back to medium shot. Text on screen: *"Small tasks pile up → big mental drain"* | **NARRATOR:** "Because those tiny tasks pile up in your brain and drain your focus on the stuff that actually matters." |

---

## CTA (0:22–0:30)

| VIDEO | AUDIO |
|---|---|
| Cut to tight shot. Speaker points at camera. Text on screen: *"FOLLOW for daily productivity tips"* Burn-in caption: *"FOLLOW for daily productivity tips"* | **NARRATOR:** "Try it for one day. You'll feel the difference by noon. Follow for more." |
| Cut to end screen — follow button animation. | **SFX:** upbeat stinger. |
```

**Shot list:** 2 camera positions (tight, medium) + 3 B-roll clips (email, filing, invoice at ~1 second each) + 1 end screen. Estimated filming time: 10-15 minutes.
